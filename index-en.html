AS TEAMS EVOLVE, MANAGEMENT MUST EVOLVE WITH THEM.

Alongside leading people, managers will direct agents: defining what they can do, monitoring delivery, correcting failures and developing their capabilities. Accountability stays with the business.

GREATER AUTONOMY DEMANDS CLEARER RULES, NOT LESS MANAGEMENT.
